Animations

Make things move, and make sure the people who cannot stand motion can turn it off.

Edit the keyframes

Change 1s to 0.3s, swap ease-in-out for steps(4), move the ball sideways.

Transitions and animations

A transition eases between two states when something changes, such as a hover. An animation runs on its own from @keyframes.

button {
  transition: transform .15s ease, box-shadow .15s ease;
}
button:hover {
  transform: translateY(-2px);
}

Move kindly

Some people get ill from motion. Always include the prefers-reduced-motion block you can see in the lab, and animate transform and opacity, which the browser can do cheaply.

Common mistake. Animating width, top or left. They force the browser to recompute layout every frame and stutter. Use transform instead.
